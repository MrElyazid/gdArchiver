Archived collections

A small index of educational archives preserved on the Internet Archive.

Collections

SABOR PC Math

A large collection of educational material. The original site hosted files on Google Drive, and many PDFs uploaded after 2022 are view-only and difficult to download directly.

23,629 files, 15,983 PDFs. Archive index

PC1.ma

An old site that also used Google Drive for file hosting. It was archived to preserve its content in case the site goes offline.

11,384 files, 7,844 PDFs. Archive index

Prepa Math Library

A collection of PDFs gathered from Google Drive, not tied to a single website. It focuses on mathematics for CPGE students.

1,932 files, 1,924 PDFs. Archive index

How the archives were built

The archives were built with gdArchiver, a custom tool that records links in a CSV file, follows the site hierarchy, and downloads the documents, including view-only PDFs, which are the most difficult to capture.
